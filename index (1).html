🔧 HOW I BUILT THIS

CodeQuest is a single-file HTML/CSS/JavaScript vocabulary review game built with help from Claude (Anthropic). The goal was to create an engaging, polished study tool for CS Principles concepts.

Vocab Source:
All 15 terms are drawn directly from a structured word bank covering Foundations, Storage, Operators & Expressions, Logic & Control, Functions, Events & Interaction, and Best Practices.

Game Mechanics:

  • 15 questions are randomly selected and shuffled each playthrough.
  • For each question, one correct term and three wrong distractors are chosen randomly from the full word bank.
  • Answer choices are shuffled so the correct answer is never in the same spot.
  • Win condition: score 12 or more out of 15.

Design Choices:
The retro-terminal aesthetic (dark background, cyan glow, scanlines, grid) was chosen to match the computing theme. Fonts are Orbitron (display) and Share Tech Mono (body) from Google Fonts, giving it a distinct sci-fi/hacker feel that differs from typical educational apps.

Technical Stack:
Pure HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript — no frameworks or libraries required. Runs entirely in the browser with zero dependencies beyond Google Fonts.
